Chorus simulation

Runs simulated choristers using the app's own chorister.js and rules.js, with realistic phone delays (20 ms out, 20 ms in, 3 ms through the air). For two phones it reports where B calls within A's cycle: 0 = together (synchrony), 0.5 = taking turns (alternation). R is how consistent that is (1 = perfectly locked, near 0 = no pattern).
